CONTACT POINT VERIFICATION AND ASSISTED KYC

The 2025 PA Directions define Contact Point Verification as physical verification of the merchant's address or place of business. Paragraph 13 permits CPV as part of the alternative due-diligence process for merchants within the stated turnover limits.

Paragraph 15 permits a non-bank PA to use authorised agents for specified Digital KYC and assisted V-CIP activities. The PA must conduct due diligence on those agents and retains ultimate responsibility. Do not describe every video call or address check as a mandatory RBI in-person verification.

PREPARE FOR A PROVIDER VERIFICATION

Use only the provider's official dashboard, application, or verified communication. Confirm the requested documents, appointment details, and purpose. Keep the original documents available where instructed, record the completion date and reference, and never disclose a password or OTP to an agent.

PRIOR COMPLAINT

Before considering the RBI Ombudsman, make a clear written complaint to the entity using its published support or grievance route. Keep proof of submission and the response. Paragraph 8 of the PA Directions requires an officer for merchant issues and a published escalation matrix, but it does not set a universal response deadline for every merchant grievance.

OMBUDSMAN ELIGIBILITY CHECK

The current Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme applies to maintainable complaints about deficiency in service by covered regulated entities. The complainant must first complain to the regulated entity and follow the current waiting-period requirements shown by RBI.

Before filing, confirm that the entity is covered, the complainant qualifies as its customer for the relevant service, the complaint is not excluded or already pending in another forum, and the filing is within the Scheme's limitation period. Use the current portal prompts rather than assuming a particular category will appear.

FILING RECORD

Prepare the complainant and entity details, prior complaint and response, chronology, transaction references, amount, requested relief, and supporting documents. State only verifiable facts. The Ombudsman process is cost-free, but MerchantOS cannot predict maintainability, compensation, or resolution time.
