--- SECTION: IN-PERSON VERIFICATION (IPV) ---

What is IPV?
In-Person Verification (IPV) is a process where a Razorpay representative (or authorized third party) verifies the identity of the merchant or business owner face-to-face or via a live video call.

When can Razorpay demand IPV?
- IPV is permitted under RBI PA Directions 2025 for high-risk merchant categories
- Razorpay may request IPV if the merchant's transaction volume exceeds a risk threshold
- IPV may be required during enhanced due diligence (EDD) for merchants with unusual activity patterns
- IPV is NOT mandatory for standard KYC submissions if document-based verification is complete

When is IPV NOT valid?
- Razorpay cannot demand IPV without citing a specific RBI provision
- IPV cannot substitute for document submission — it is an additional verification, not a replacement
- If Razorpay demands IPV without justification, the merchant can formally dispute this under RBI PA Directions 2025, Para 3.2

What to do if IPV is requested:
1. Ask Razorpay to specify the exact regulatory provision requiring IPV
2. Schedule the IPV via Razorpay's official video KYC link (provided in their email)
3. Keep a record of: date, time, Razorpay agent ID, and outcome of the IPV call
4. If IPV fails due to technical issues on Razorpay's side, request a reschedule in writing

Documents to keep ready during IPV:
- Original PAN Card
- Original Aadhaar Card
- Any business registration certificate (Udyam, GST, MOA/AOA, Partnership Deed)
- Bank statement (last 3 months) if requested

IPV TAT (Turnaround Time):
- Razorpay must complete IPV review within 3 business days of completion
- If IPV result is not communicated within 3 business days, escalate to Grievance Officer

--- SECTION: RBI INTEGRATED OMBUDSMAN COMPLAINT FILING ---

What is the RBI Integrated Ombudsman?
The RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021 provides a single-window redressal mechanism for complaints against banks and payment service providers including Payment Aggregators (PAs) like Razorpay.

URL to file complaint: https://cms.rbi.org.in

When can you escalate to RBI Ombudsman?
- After 30 days of complaint to Razorpay with no resolution
- If Razorpay rejects your complaint and you are not satisfied with the response
- If Razorpay does not respond within the stipulated time (5 business days for standard issues, 4 hours for settlement holds)
- Complaint is FREE OF CHARGE — no fees required

How to file a complaint with RBI Ombudsman (step by step):
1. Visit https://cms.rbi.org.in
2. Click "File a Complaint"
3. Select "Payment Aggregator" as the entity type
4. Enter Razorpay as the regulated entity
5. Provide:
   - Your merchant ID
   - Date the problem started
   - Description of the issue (be specific — cite hold reason, RBI provision violated)
   - All prior communication with Razorpay (ticket numbers, emails)
   - Amount involved (if settlement hold)
6. Attach all supporting documents
7. Submit — you receive an acknowledgment number

What information to include in your complaint:
- Razorpay's failure to provide written notice within 24 hours (if applicable)
- Specific RBI provision being violated (e.g., Para 4.3(ii) for wrong doc demand)
- TAT violation (if settlement held beyond 2 business days without reason)
- Excess fee charged (with calculation)
- All merchant-side documents already submitted

RBI Ombudsman powers:
- Can direct Razorpay to settle the complaint within 30 days
- Can award compensation up to Rs 20 lakh for consequential losses
- Decision is binding on Razorpay if merchant accepts

Complaint TAT at Ombudsman:
- RBI Ombudsman must acknowledge within 3 days
- Resolution within 30 days of receipt of all documents

--- SECTION: GRIEVANCE OFFICER ESCALATION ---

Razorpay Grievance Officer contact:
- Email: grievance.officer@razorpay.com
- Response time: Within 30 days (mandated by RBI)

When to escalate to Grievance Officer:
- After 5 business days with no resolution from Razorpay support
- Before escalating to RBI Ombudsman

What to include in Grievance Officer email:
- Original support ticket number
- Date filed and current status
- Specific RBI provision being violated
- Amount being disputed (if applicable)
- Request for written justification for the hold
