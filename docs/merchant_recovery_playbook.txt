MERCHANT FUND RECOVERY AND ESCALATION PLAYBOOK
Source: RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2021 + RBI PA Directions 2025 + Legal Precedents

SECTION A: UNDERSTANDING YOUR RIGHTS AS A MERCHANT

Under Indian law, a Payment Aggregator (PA) like Razorpay is a regulated entity under the RBI Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007. You, as a merchant, have enforceable legal rights.

YOUR CORE RIGHTS:
1. Right to written communication: Any hold or freeze must be communicated in writing within 24 hours (RBI PA Directions 2025, Section 5.2).
2. Right to know the reason: Razorpay must tell you EXACTLY why your funds are held, citing the specific regulatory provision.
3. Right to resolution timeline: Razorpay must give you a clear timeline for resolving the hold.
4. Right to dispute document demands: If Razorpay asks for a document not required by RBI, you can formally dispute it.
5. Right to escalation: If unresolved in 30 days, you can escalate to the RBI Ombudsman at zero cost.

SECTION B: STEP-BY-STEP RECOVERY GUIDE

STEP 1 - DOCUMENT THE HOLD (Day 0-1):
- Screenshot your Razorpay dashboard showing the account status
- Note the exact date the hold started and the amount being held
- Save all emails from Razorpay regarding the hold
- Calculate the financial impact: held amount x daily revenue loss

STEP 2 - SUBMIT A FORMAL SUPPORT TICKET (Day 1-2):
- Go to https://razorpay.com/support/ and create a ticket
- Subject line: "Settlement Hold - Merchant ID [XXX] - Request for Written Explanation"
- Attach: all supporting documents, RBI regulation citations
- Request: written reason, list of required actions, resolution timeline
- Keep ticket number for escalation reference

STEP 3 - SUBMIT REQUIRED DOCUMENTS (Day 2-5):
- Submit exactly what RBI requires for your merchant category
- Do NOT submit documents not required (like GST if you are unregistered)
- If Razorpay insists on GST from an unregistered entity, cite RBI Para 4.3(ii) formally
- Upload via Razorpay dashboard or email to kyc@razorpay.com

STEP 4 - ESCALATE TO GRIEVANCE OFFICER (Day 5-7, if unresolved):
- Email: grievance.officer@razorpay.com
- Subject: "Formal Grievance - Unresolved Settlement Hold - Merchant ID [XXX]"
- Include: original ticket number, date of submission, all correspondence, financial impact statement
- Per RBI, Grievance Officer must acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days

STEP 5 - RBI INTEGRATED OMBUDSMAN (Day 30+, if unresolved):
- URL: https://cms.rbi.org.in
- File under: Payment and Settlement System complaint
- PA Name: Razorpay Software Private Limited
- Attach: all previous correspondence, Razorpay ticket number, Grievance Officer email
- RBI will investigate and can order fund release with compensation

SECTION C: SAMPLE COMPLAINT LANGUAGE

For GST Dispute (Unregistered Merchant):
"I am an unregistered sole proprietor and hereby dispute Razorpay's demand for a GST Certificate. Per RBI PA Master Directions 2025, Section 4.3(ii), unregistered sole proprietors are NOT required to provide GSTIN. I am providing the alternative documents listed in Para 4.3(ii). Kindly confirm acceptance within 48 hours or I will escalate to the RBI Ombudsman."

For Unreasonable Hold Duration:
"My account has been on hold for [X] days without resolution. Per RBI PA Directions 2025, Section 5.4, KYC holds must be resolved within 7 business days of complete document submission. I submitted all documents on [date]. This constitutes a violation of RBI-mandated TAT. I request immediate release of Rs. [amount] or written justification citing the specific regulatory basis for continued hold."

For Missing Written Communication:
"I have not received written communication explaining the reason for my account hold as required by RBI PA Directions 2025, Section 5.2. Failure to provide this within 24 hours of placing a hold constitutes a regulatory violation. I request immediate written communication or I will file a complaint with the RBI Integrated Ombudsman."

SECTION D: FINANCIAL IMPACT CALCULATION

How to Calculate Your Loss from a Settlement Hold:
- Daily revenue = (Monthly transaction volume) / 30
- Revenue blocked = Daily revenue x Number of days held
- Opportunity cost = Lost sales due to customers getting payment errors
- Example: Rs. 3 lakh/month volume = Rs. 10,000/day. A 15-day hold = Rs. 1.5 lakh blocked.

In the RBI Ombudsman complaint, include this calculation. The Ombudsman can award:
- Release of held funds
- Interest on delayed settlements (at prevailing bank rate)
- Compensation up to Rs. 20 lakh for mental agony and financial loss

SECTION E: SPECIFIC SCENARIOS AND RESOLUTIONS

SCENARIO: Razorpay demands GST from a freelancer earning below Rs. 20 lakh/year.
RESOLUTION: GST registration is optional below this threshold. Cite Income Tax Act, GST Act threshold, AND RBI PA Directions 2025 Para 4.3(ii). Razorpay cannot mandate registration with a government body as a pre-condition for payment services.

SCENARIO: Merchant's account is suspended without any email notification.
RESOLUTION: This is a direct violation of RBI Section 5.2 (must notify within 24 hours). File an emergency complaint with both Razorpay Grievance Officer and RBI Ombudsman simultaneously. Request compensation for the notification violation.

SCENARIO: Chargeback ratio slightly above threshold due to a single fraudulent customer.
RESOLUTION: Provide documentation showing the fraud was reported, customer communication logs, and police complaint if filed. Request Razorpay exclude the fraudulent transaction from ratio calculation. If refused, escalate citing that the ratio threshold exists to prevent systemic fraud, not penalize one-off victim merchants.

SCENARIO: Transaction volume spike due to legitimate seasonal sale.
RESOLUTION: Provide advance written notice of future sales events to Razorpay's risk team. For current holds, provide: marketing campaign proof, invoice samples, customer list, fulfillment records. A business event letter on company letterhead is sufficient evidence.
