SOURCE BOUNDARY

This file summarises public documentation and is not a copy of a merchant's individual contract or dashboard. Product eligibility, documents, settlement schedules, pricing, and risk controls can vary. The current dashboard, provider communication, and merchant agreement should be treated as case-specific evidence.

KYC DOCUMENTS

Razorpay publishes a current KYC document page organised by business type. It also states that additional certifications may be requested depending on the line of business. Merchants should use the current provider page and dashboard checklist rather than a static MerchantOS list as the final requirement.

Current public material describes CKYCR consent and verification flows. It distinguishes registered businesses, individuals, proprietorships, partnerships, companies, trusts, and other entity types. Document requests may include PAN, identity or address proof, entity documents, authorised-signatory evidence, beneficial-owner information, or category-specific licences.

If a requested document is unavailable, ask support which current policy, risk condition, or legal rule requires it and whether an accepted alternative exists. Do not claim that the RBI PA Directions themselves prohibit a GST request; paragraph 13 points to KYC due diligence but does not provide a merchant-type GST checklist.

SETTLEMENT SCHEDULE

Razorpay's public settlement documentation describes T+2 working days as the standard domestic settlement cycle while noting that the schedule can vary based on business vertical, risk factors, bank approval, and the schedule shown in the dashboard. International settlement timing is shown in the dashboard and depends on applicable requirements.

MerchantOS therefore treats T+2 as a configurable provider expectation, not a universal statutory RBI deadline. Bank holidays are not automatically modelled by the local audit; verify exceptions against the dashboard timeline.

SETTLEMENT HOLDS

Razorpay's dashboard documentation says a settlement may be placed on hold when risk is detected with payments or the account. The merchant is notified on the dashboard and is directed to contact support to enable held settlements. Preserve the dashboard notice and all provider correspondence.

RECONCILIATION

Razorpay's settlement breakdown includes payment amount, adjustments, tax, fees, and settlement amount. Compare the exported report with the contracted pricing and dashboard schedule. MerchantOS fee rates are operator-configured estimates and must not be presented as the provider's binding price list.

ESCALATION RECORD

Start with the provider's current support and merchant-grievance channels. Retain ticket numbers, dates, responses, transaction IDs, bank statements, and the relevant dashboard timeline. Verify any Ombudsman or legal route separately before submitting a generated draft.
