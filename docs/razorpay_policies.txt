RAZORPAY MERCHANT POLICIES AND KYC REQUIREMENTS
Source: Razorpay Official Support Documentation | Last Updated: August 2026

RAZORPAY KYC COMPLIANCE OVERVIEW

Razorpay, as a Payment Aggregator (PA) licensed by the RBI, is required to verify the identity of all merchants on its platform. This process, known as Know Your Customer (KYC), is mandated under the RBI Master Direction on Payment Aggregators 2025.

RAZORPAY ACCOUNT STATES AND WHAT THEY MEAN

Live Disabled: Your account exists but all payment acceptance is paused. This is the most common state for KYC holds. You cannot accept new payments but can view your dashboard.

Payment Disabled: Only payment links and payment buttons are disabled. Your payment pages may still work. Usually a partial hold for minor compliance gaps.

Settlement on Hold: You can still accept payments but funds are not being settled to your bank account. The money accumulates in Razorpay's escrow. This is common for risk-related holds.

Account Suspended: Full account freeze. No payments, no settlements, no access to funds. This is the most severe state and requires immediate action.

Under Review / Pending KYC: Razorpay is actively reviewing your submitted documents. This state typically lasts 2-7 business days.

RAZORPAY KYC REQUIREMENTS BY MERCHANT TYPE

1. SOLE PROPRIETOR - UNREGISTERED:
   - PAN Card of the proprietor (mandatory)
   - Aadhaar Card of the proprietor (mandatory)
   - Bank account in proprietor name (mandatory)
   - Business proof - ANY ONE of: Last 6 months bank statement, Utility bill in business name, Udyam registration, Self-declaration letter
   - IMPORTANT: GST Certificate is NOT mandatory for unregistered sole proprietors per RBI PA Directions 2025 Para 4.3(ii). Razorpay's internal policy cannot override RBI regulations.

2. SOLE PROPRIETOR - REGISTERED (with GST or Udyam):
   - PAN Card of the proprietor (mandatory)
   - Aadhaar Card of the proprietor (mandatory)
   - GST Certificate OR Udyam Registration (mandatory for registered proprietors)
   - Bank account in business or proprietor name

3. PARTNERSHIP FIRM:
   - PAN Card of the firm (mandatory)
   - Partnership Deed - registered or unregistered (mandatory)
   - PAN Card and Aadhaar of each partner (mandatory)
   - Bank account in the firm name (mandatory)
   - Any one of: GST Certificate, 6-month bank statement

4. PRIVATE LIMITED COMPANY:
   - Certificate of Incorporation from MCA (mandatory)
   - Company PAN Card (mandatory)
   - Memorandum of Association (MOA) (mandatory)
   - Articles of Association (AOA) (mandatory)
   - Board Resolution authorizing the signatory (mandatory)
   - PAN Card and Aadhaar of all Directors (mandatory)
   - Any one of: GST Certificate, latest audited financials

5. NGO / TRUST / SOCIETY:
   - Trust Deed or Certificate of Registration (mandatory)
   - PAN Card of the entity (mandatory)
   - PAN and Aadhaar of trustees or governing body (mandatory)
   - 12A Registration Certificate (for income tax exemption) recommended
   - 80G Certificate if applicable
   - GST is generally NOT applicable to NGOs and cannot be mandated

6. INDIVIDUAL FREELANCER:
   - PAN Card (mandatory)
   - Aadhaar Card (mandatory)
   - Bank account in individual name (mandatory)
   - Work portfolio, client contracts, or 6-month bank statement as business proof
   - GST Certificate: Only mandatory if annual turnover exceeds Rs. 20 lakh (Rs. 10 lakh for special category states). Below this threshold, GST registration is optional and cannot be demanded.

RAZORPAY SETTLEMENT TIMELINES

Standard Settlement Cycle: T+2 business days for most merchants (T = day of transaction).
Instant Settlements: Available for Rs. 9/transaction for eligible merchants with low-risk profile.
Manual Settlement: Available on request outside normal settlement cycle.
International Payments: T+5 to T+7 business days due to correspondent banking requirements.

Settlement Holds - When Razorpay Can Hold Funds:
1. Pending KYC: Funds held until KYC documents verified. Maximum hold: 30 days after KYC email.
2. High Chargeback Ratio: Razorpay monitors chargeback ratios relative to Card Network programs (Visa VDMP ~0.9%, Mastercard ECP ~1.0%) and internal risk policy (~0.75% monitoring threshold).
3. Fraud Detection: Automated systems flag suspicious transaction patterns.
4. Refund Reserve: Razorpay may hold a rolling reserve (typically 5-10% of daily settlement) for 90-120 days for high-risk merchants.

WHAT RAZORPAY CANNOT DO

Under RBI PA Directions 2025, Razorpay cannot:
- Demand GST Certificate from merchants who are not required to register for GST
- Hold settlements for more than 30 days without written justification
- Block accounts without notifying the merchant in writing within 24 hours
- Charge merchants for KYC document verification
- Refuse to provide the reason for a hold in writing
- Permanently freeze accounts without due process

RAZORPAY GRIEVANCE ESCALATION PROCESS

Level 1 - Support Ticket: Submit at https://razorpay.com/support/
Response time: 1-2 business days for standard queries, 4 hours for settlement holds.

Level 2 - Grievance Officer: Email grievance.officer@razorpay.com
Mandatory if Level 1 not resolved within 5 business days.
Per RBI requirement, Grievance Officer must respond within 30 days.

Level 3 - RBI Integrated Ombudsman: https://cms.rbi.org.in
Available if Razorpay has not resolved the complaint within 30 days.
Free of charge. RBI typically responds within 30-45 days.

RAZORPAY CHARGEBACK PROCESS

A chargeback occurs when a customer disputes a transaction with their bank. The bank reverses the payment and Razorpay debits it from the merchant's account.

Chargeback Ratio: (Total chargeback amount / Total transaction amount) x 100
Safe threshold: Below 0.75% per platform policy and below Card Network thresholds.
Razorpay monitoring threshold: 0.75% - holds may be placed above this.

To fight a chargeback, merchants should provide:
- Proof of delivery or service completion
- Customer communication confirming receipt
- Invoice and order details
- IP address and device fingerprint from Razorpay dashboard
- Refund policy that was visible at time of purchase

TRANSACTION VOLUME SPIKE POLICY

When Razorpay detects an unusual spike in transaction volume (typically 200% or more increase vs. 30-day average), an automated risk hold may be placed.

To resolve a volume spike hold, provide:
- Business explanation for the volume increase (marketing campaign, seasonal sale, etc.)
- Sample invoices showing legitimate orders
- Customer list with contact details (for verification)
- Letter on company letterhead explaining the business event
